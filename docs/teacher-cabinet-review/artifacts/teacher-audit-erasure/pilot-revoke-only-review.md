# Fizika pilot: revoke-only scope release (design, not an erasure API)

Pinned project source: `3dbb714f2f758c7dbe03548a71c161718cf9cb53`. Owner decision: transferring responsibility is optional. Revoking a Teacher's management of a group must leave the group (including when nobody currently has Teacher scope), roster, other managers, tests, submissions, results, saved answers, and original deadlines unchanged. It neither removes Student entitlement nor itself deletes the account.

## Exact boundary

1. Begin under a separately reviewed, current Owner authorization for one real group/Teacher pair (or separately authorized Teacher self-release; no handoff recipient is inferred). The pair must come from existing canonical identities and group state, not a request-supplied role or a fixture. Share the canonical Clerk advisory → identity mapping → person lock ordering used by deletion; with multiple people, use existing ordered person locks and check all affected participants. Verify active actor/target, current provider liveness/lease, and no deletion freeze; every scope/provision/handoff writer must follow the same exclusion protocol. Do not add an Owner or global grant.
2. Under that same transaction, get and lock the exact `staff_group_scope(person_id, group_id)` row and validate release eligibility. Prove the write set contains just this exact management edge and nothing in memberships or Student test grants. A revoke-only operation may mark the edge revoked to stop access; if this reviewed step is also expected to satisfy the existing account-deletion *absence* guard, it must support removing the exact edge physically, not merely toggling it. No cascade or inferred cleanup. Never clear staff roles, other subject/group scopes, approver records, or broader authorities as a side effect. Optional handoff is a distinct approved operation checking the recipient's current verified authority and may not move any Student.
3. Reauthorize at replay/commit. Fresh command: record only under the reviewed audit lifecycle, in the same rollback boundary; do not omit the receipt/audit just because there are no legacy rows. Existing command: if it would stay linked to the departing person, typed provenance and response allowlist are required; uncertain or conflicting payload, identity, scope, receipt, or missing completeness fails closed. An already absent edge is not proof of this operation's success; do not revoke a later regrant on old replay, reconstruct evidence, allocate a successor, or call deletion as a consequence. Prove before mutation that a departing-actor replay is barred by current authorization/deletion and that any surviving actor's affected command (including Owner provisioning) cannot execute again against the same unavailable target, even after a receipt is erased. If a surviving route allocates a fresh resource or restores scope on receipt absence, it must instead reject or retain only an independently approved non-identifying terminal gate; this design does not supply one.
4. Existing `prepare_account_deletion` still requires exact Student-only roles and absence of all listed staff references (SQL lines 69–93). Mere scope revocation, active-group absence, or a presumed zero-row RLS result is not sufficient. The complete authenticated locked check must cover other staff rows and paired receipts/audit actor/typed targets plus reviewed payload/key/response references. Owner-attributed provisioning can still identify the Teacher in payload even if audit actor and group target differ; do not confuse the current narrow prepare predicate with full erasure. Unresolved references retain the current rejection, including when deferred reciprocal FKs or immutability prevent exact paired handling. Do not weaken the old locks, guards, or provider recovery path.
5. Separate operator/owner-reviewed changes are needed for actual permission/storage boundary, immutable-history lifecycle, surviving replay proof, and account offboarding beyond group scope. No expiration, purge authorization, production activation, or deletion of a real account is inferred. No operation in this note is mounted.
